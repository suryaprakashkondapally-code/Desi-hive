About Desi Hive 🐝

The Global Digital Village for People of Indian Origin

Desi Hive is a unified, hyper-local ecosystem designed to connect the 30-million-strong Indian diaspora. Spanning across all 7 continents, Desi Hive organizes the community into "Global Hoods" to facilitate safety, resource sharing, and cultural continuity.

🚨

Global SOS

A proprietary 10-second countdown system that broadcasts emergency alerts to every "Hive Member" within a 5km radius, mapped in real-time.

🩸

Rakt Sewa

Life-Saving Matching: A dedicated network for blood donation that works across borders, connecting donors with seekers instantly and securely.

💼

Hiring Hive

A segmented community job board allowing users to hire trusted talent from within, ranging from local neighborhood tasks to global corporate roles.

🛒

Bazaar & AI

Buy, sell, or barter traditional items locally. Guided by "Dost AI", your virtual neighbor who understands platform rules and cultural nuances.

🌍 Connecting the Indosphere

We aim to solve the "Immigrant Isolation" problem. By digitizing neighborhood trust, we build a world where no person of Indian origin is ever truly a stranger.
